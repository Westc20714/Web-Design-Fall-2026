Life on Campus

Campus life at SUNY New Paltz offers students many ways to get involved and build connections. From student organizations and campus events to athletics and recreational activities, there are opportunities for students with a wide range of interests.

The campus environment combines academics with social, cultural, and recreational experiences, giving students opportunities to learn and grow both inside and outside the classroom.

Health & Wellness

Maintaining a healthy balance between academics and personal life is an important part of the college experience. Students can take advantage of wellness and recreational resources available on campus.

The Hudson Valley Outdoors

One of the unique aspects of SUNY New Paltz is its location in the Hudson Valley. The surrounding area provides students with opportunities to enjoy nature and explore the outdoors.

  • Hiking and walking trails
  • Scenic views of the Hudson Valley
  • Outdoor recreation
  • Exploring the Shawangunk Ridge
  • Local arts, culture, and community events

A Great Place to Call Home

New Paltz, New York

The university is located in the village of New Paltz, surrounded by the natural scenery of the Hudson Valley. Students can enjoy the convenience of a college town while also having access to outdoor recreation and nearby communities.
