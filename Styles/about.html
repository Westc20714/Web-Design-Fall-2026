About the University

The State University of New York at New Paltz, commonly known as SUNY New Paltz, is a public university located in New Paltz, New York. The university is part of the State University of New York system and provides students with opportunities to explore a wide range of academic disciplines.

SUNY New Paltz combines a liberal arts education with professional programs, creative disciplines, research opportunities, and hands-on learning experiences. Students can participate in organizations, activities, athletics, and other aspects of campus life.

A University with History

SUNY New Paltz has a long history of education and has evolved throughout the years to meet the changing needs of its students and community.

Today, the university continues to build on that tradition by providing students with opportunities to learn, conduct research, develop professional skills, and become active members of their communities.

Located in New Paltz, New York

SUNY New Paltz is located in the Hudson Valley of New York. The area provides students with access to a combination of small-town community, outdoor recreation, arts and culture, and nearby cities.

The university's location near the Shawangunk Ridge also provides opportunities to enjoy hiking, climbing, and other outdoor activities.
