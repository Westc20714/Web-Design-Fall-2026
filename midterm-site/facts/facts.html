Did You Know?

Explore some fascinating things about our feline friends.

👂

Amazing Hearing

Cats can hear many high-frequency sounds that humans cannot, helping them detect small animals.

👃

Unique Noses

Every cat has a distinctive nose pattern, similar to how human fingerprints differ.

😴

Sleep Experts

Cats commonly sleep around 12–16 hours a day, although kittens and older cats may sleep more.

🐈

Whisker Sensors

Whiskers are sensitive hairs that help cats sense nearby objects and changes in their surroundings.

🗣️

Different Meows

Cats communicate through sounds, posture, facial expressions, and scent. Some are especially vocal.

🌀

Righting Reflex

Cats have a righting reflex that can help them rotate their bodies while falling. Falls can still cause serious injuries, so keep windows secure.

🧭

Excellent Balance

Cats have flexible bodies and a strong sense of balance that helps them move across narrow surfaces.

🐾

Quiet Walkers

Cats have soft paw pads that help them move quietly when exploring their surroundings.

🏛️

Ancient Companions

Cats have lived alongside humans for thousands of years, including in ancient Egyptian society.
