Meet the Cats

Explore these popular cat breeds.

Domestic Shorthair

Adaptable Playful

A popular mixed-ancestry cat found in many colors and patterns. Each cat has its own personality.

Coat: Short and varied
Personality: Varies by individual

Persian

Calm Fluffy

Famous for a long, luxurious coat and round face. Persians benefit from frequent grooming.

Coat: Long and thick
Personality: Often gentle

British Shorthair

Easygoing Independent

Recognizable by its round face, sturdy build, and dense, plush coat.

Coat: Short and dense
Personality: Often relaxed

Siamese

Social Vocal

Known for blue eyes, color-point markings, and a tendency to communicate with their people.

Coat: Short and sleek
Personality: Often outgoing

Maine Coon

Large Sociable

One of the largest domestic cat breeds, with a long coat, tufted ears, and bushy tail.

Coat: Long and shaggy
Personality: Often friendly

Ragdoll

Affectionate Gentle

A blue-eyed breed with a semi-long coat. Many enjoy companionship and relaxed play.

Coat: Semi-long
Personality: Often sociable

🐾 Choosing the Right Cat

Every cat is an individual. Think about your lifestyle, available space, grooming time, and how much interaction you can provide. Consider adopting from a shelter, too! Spend time getting to know a cat before deciding whether you are a good match for one another.
