Academics

Academic Excellence

SUNY New Paltz offers students opportunities to study a wide range of subjects through undergraduate and graduate programs. Students can explore their interests while developing the knowledge and skills needed for future careers and continued education.

With a combination of classroom learning, creative projects, research, internships, and other experiences, students can build an education that goes beyond the traditional classroom.
